# 10-Minute Demo Script

This script is designed for a live walkthrough of the RevenueMind. It covers all four actors in under 10 minutes.

## Demo Objective

Show that the system can:
- ingest business files
- turn them into usable pricing controls
- generate an explainable recommendation
- route risky pricing for approval
- show strategic visibility for executives
- keep admin governance and traceability

## 1:30 To 4:30 - Sales Manager Quote Workflow

Login as `salesmanager@gmail.com`.

Open:
- `/sales`
- an existing quote or deal workspace

Say:
`The Sales Manager does not need to read every policy or price list manually. The system combines all active business controls and recommends the best action.`

Generate a recommendation and show:
- recommended price
- quick why summary
- true margin
- leakage control summary
- campaign eligibility
- pricebook enforcement
- contract pricing summary
- market comparison
- next best action

Say:
`The recommendation is grounded in uploaded policy rules, pricebooks, contract logic, and competitor data.`

If approval is required, request approval and say:
`The quote now moves into approval governance with full business context, not just a number.`
